💰 Personal Expense Tracker

📊 Your Smart Personal Finance Companion

Personal Expense Tracker is a simple and interactive web application designed to help users manage their daily finances by tracking income, monitoring expenses, setting budgets, and organizing financial records.

It aims to solve a common everyday problem:

«"Where is my money going?"»

Instead of manually maintaining expense records, users can manage their financial activities through one simple interface.

---

🎯 The Problem

Managing personal finances can become difficult without proper tracking.

People commonly face:

- 💸 Difficulty keeping track of daily expenses
- 📉 Lack of awareness about spending habits
- 📝 Manual maintenance of income and expense records
- 🎯 Difficulty setting and following budgets
- 📊 Limited understanding of overall financial activity

Without a proper tracking system, it becomes harder to understand where money is being spent.

---

💡 The Solution

Personal Expense Tracker provides a simple digital platform to organize and monitor personal finances.

The application connects:

Income → Expenses → Categories → Budget → Transaction History → Financial Overview

Users can record transactions, organize spending, monitor budgets, view their financial activity, and export their financial data.

---

✨ Key Features

💵 Income and Expense Management

- Add income transactions
- Record daily expenses
- Manage financial entries
- Keep income and expenses organized

📂 Expense Categorization

- Organize transactions by category
- Make expense records easier to understand
- Track different types of spending

📊 Financial Dashboard

- View total income
- View total expenses
- Check available balance
- Get an overview of financial activity

🎯 Budget Management

- Set personal budgets
- Monitor spending against budget limits
- Keep track of budget usage

📜 Transaction History

- View recorded transactions
- Maintain financial records
- Review previous income and expenses

📤 Export Financial Data

- Export financial records
- Keep a separate copy of transaction data
- Use exported data for personal record-keeping
